# Year 2/3 Space Colony Designers

**Unit title:** Space Colony Designers: Robotic Construction for Other Planets  
**Semester:** Semester 2, 2026  
**Learning area:** Technologies  
**Subject:** Design and Technologies  
**Class:** Year 2/Year 3 multi-age class  
**Duration:** 18 weeks, 1 lesson per week, plus showcase preparation  
**Project link:** Advanced Manufacturing: Future Skills Program  
**Technologies contexts:** Engineering Principles and Systems; Materials and Technologies Specialisations  
**Final showcase product:** Group robotic 3D print construction machine prototype and explanation, plus individual Minecraft Education space base with annotated plan and reflection.

## Unit rationale

This unit replaces the original Naturally Brave context while keeping the intended Semester 2 Design Technologies focus for the Year 2/3 class. The new context strengthens the school advanced manufacturing project by placing students in the role of young designers and manufacturing engineers who need to solve a future problem: how could humans build safe, useful and sustainable settlements beyond Earth?

Students investigate human needs, space settlement challenges, 3D printing, robotic construction, materials, structures and sustainable design. They communicate ideas through sketches, annotated technical drawings, Makers Empire prototypes, Minecraft Education builds and showcase presentations.

The unit deliberately prioritises the Design Technologies process: investigating, defining, ideating, prototyping, testing, improving, evaluating and presenting.

## Big idea

People design technologies to meet human needs. Future space settlements require engineered and sustainable solutions for shelter, food, energy, water, transport, communication, community spaces and safe construction.

## Essential question

How might we design a robotic 3D print construction machine and space settlement that help humans live safely and sustainably beyond Earth?

## Design challenge

Design a robotic 3D printer that could construct buildings on another planet, then design a space settlement that the construction robot could help build.

## Design thinking structure

- **Empathise:** Explore the needs of people living in a space settlement and the limits of building in an extreme environment.
- **Define:** Co-develop the design brief and design criteria for a robotic construction machine and later for a space settlement.
- **Ideate:** Generate many possible robot designs, compare options and select a preferred concept.
- **Prototype:** Create annotated drawings and a 3D model in Makers Empire. Later build the settlement in Minecraft Education.
- **Test and improve:** Use feedback and design criteria to improve the robot and settlement.

## Australian Curriculum alignment

The curriculum alignment is written for a Year 2/3 multi-age class. Year 2 students are judged against the end Year 2 Design Technologies achievement standard. Year 3 students are working towards the end Year 4 Design Technologies achievement standard. Digital tools are used, but the assessment evidence is mainly Design Technologies evidence.

### Content description focus

- Describe how forces and the properties of materials affect function in a product or system.
- Explore needs or opportunities for designing and test materials, components, tools, equipment and processes needed to create designed solutions.
- Generate and communicate design ideas and decisions using technical terms and graphical representation techniques, including digital tools.
- Select and use materials, components, tools, equipment and techniques to safely make designed solutions.
- Use given or co-developed design criteria including sustainability to evaluate design ideas and solutions.
- Sequence steps to individually and collaboratively make designed solutions.

## Learning sequence

1. Tuning in: What is advanced manufacturing and why build in space?
2. Investigate and define: User needs and design criteria
3. Ideate 1: Robot concept generation
4. Ideate 2: Select and justify a preferred robot design
5. Technical drawing 1: Labels, arrows and annotations
6. Technical drawing 2: Final annotated robot design
7. Makers Empire skills: 3D printer and robot components
8. Makers Empire prototype 1: Robot base and structure
9. Makers Empire prototype 2: Print arm, nozzle and construction function
10. Makers Empire prototype 3: Improve scale, strength and communication
11. Engineering Expo: Peer feedback
12. Improve robot design and prepare showcase evidence
13. Space Settlement Challenge: Needs, zones and systems
14. Settlement planning: Annotated map and construction sequence
15. Minecraft build 1: Landform, zones and main structures
16. Minecraft build 2: Sustainable systems
17. Minecraft build 3: Improve, label and prepare presentation
18. Showcase and reflection

## Assessment overview

### Task 1: Design a Space Construction Robot

Group task with individual teacher observations and short individual reflection. Evidence includes annotated robot drawing, Makers Empire prototype, improvement evidence and showcase explanation.

### Task 2: Space Settlement Designer

Individual task. Evidence includes annotated settlement plan, Minecraft Education space base, screenshots, reflection and showcase explanation.
